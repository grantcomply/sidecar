## Extended Camelot matching

Serato Sidecar now suggests a much wider pool of harmonically compatible
tracks. Previously only identical, adjacent, and relative (A/B) keys were
offered — the suggestion engine now also recommends:

- **Diagonal** moves — change key and mood together
- **Energy boost / drop** — ±2 steps on the Camelot wheel
- **Semitone** shifts
- **Related** keys — the loosest usable harmonic move

Each suggestion shows a match percentage that drops as the harmonic
relationship gets looser, so the safest mixes still stand out at a glance.
The match-percentage badge now uses five colour bands instead of three,
and hovering a suggestion shows the type of harmonic move (Perfect match,
Energy ±2, Diagonal, and so on).

The suggestion list cap was raised from 30 to 60 to make room for the
wider pool.
